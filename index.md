# SilkWheel

Smooth mouse wheel scrolling for macOS.

SilkWheel turns ordinary mouse wheel steps into smooth scrolling. It lives in the menu bar and includes adjustable speed, separate mouse wheel direction, Back/Forward buttons, per-app exclusions, and optional launch at login.

**SilkWheel Pro** adds Precise and Floaty feels, acceleration, and custom mouse button actions through a one-time purchase.

Requires macOS 26 or later. Trackpads and Magic Mouse scrolling pass through unchanged.
