# SilkWheel Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 11, 2026_

SilkWheel is a macOS menu bar utility that makes mouse wheel scrolling smooth.

## Data we collect

None. SilkWheel does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no network access of its own.

## What stays on your Mac

- **Settings** — on/off state, feel, speed, direction and the list of excluded apps are saved in the app's local preferences on your Mac and never leave it.
- **Scroll events** — SilkWheel processes mouse wheel events in memory to replace them with smooth scrolling. Nothing is recorded or saved. Keystrokes and screen contents are never read.
- **Excluded apps** — to check whether the window under the pointer belongs to an excluded app, SilkWheel reads only window position and owner app identifier provided by macOS, not window contents.

## Purchases

SilkWheel Pro is sold through the Mac App Store. Payment is handled entirely by Apple; we receive no payment or personal information. Apple's privacy policy applies: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

## Permissions

SilkWheel asks for the macOS Accessibility permission only to post smooth scroll events. You can revoke it at any time in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility.

## Children

SilkWheel collects no data from anyone, including children.
