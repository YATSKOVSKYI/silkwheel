# SilkWheel Support

Contact [dmytroyatskovskyi@outlook.com](mailto:dmytroyatskovskyi@outlook.com) for help with SilkWheel.

Include your macOS version, SilkWheel version, mouse model, and a description of the issue. Do not send passwords, payment details, or other sensitive information.

## Getting started

1. Open SilkWheel and click its wave icon in the menu bar.
2. Choose **Allow Access…** and enable SilkWheel in **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility**.
3. Scroll with a mouse wheel. Trackpads and Magic Mouse scrolling keep their standard behavior.
4. Hold **Option** while scrolling to temporarily use standard scrolling.

## Settings

Open **Settings…** (⌘,) from the menu bar panel to adjust scrolling, mouse buttons, excluded apps, and Pro.

Use **Excluded apps** for games or other apps that should keep their standard scrolling.

## SilkWheel Pro

SilkWheel Pro is a one-time in-app purchase. It unlocks Precise and Floaty feels, scroll acceleration, and custom actions for extra mouse buttons.

To restore an existing purchase, open **Settings → Pro → Restore Purchase** using the Apple Account that made the purchase. Apple handles purchases and billing through the Mac App Store.

## Requirements

macOS 26 or later, and a mouse with a scroll wheel.
